What this build assumes +

No ground mask, horizon alignment, or geometry form is required. The engine tests plane hypotheses using optical flow and separates rotation from translation. It is not general 3-D SLAM.

A visible, textured, approximately planar ground surface is needed. A side window is supported in the geometric model, but reflections, nearby moving vehicles, darkness and strong tilt can defeat it. A plane consistent with the images is not proof that it is the ground.

Lens field of view starts at 76° with 12° standard deviation. Camera height starts with a broad log-normal assumption (median 1.5 m, log standard deviation 0.55). These are engineering priors, not detected camera specifications.

COCO-SSD may provide person, car or bus boxes. Their assumed size distributions and ground-contact geometry provide tentative scale clues. Class-level bias is retained; seeing the same object repeatedly does not create independent rulers.

The interval samples geometry, lens and scale uncertainty. It is not a validated 95% coverage guarantee. A dash means the latest speed cannot be resolved; it never means zero or a GPS fallback.

Camera motion includes hand movement. Use a stable, passenger-operated mount. Keep the tab visible. Camera and location stop when you leave or background the page.

No image, coordinates, or sensor readings are uploaded by this app. External hosts serve the model assets; your browser/OS manages location access. Exported logs stay local unless you share them.
