Assumptions & controls +

This is an experiment, not a validated speedometer. The camera view shows an approximate estimate, not GPS speed. A dash means the visual evidence is inadequate.

The app tracks features, proposes ground-like planes, and separates rotation from translation. It does not recognize roads or object sizes semantically. Ground, fixed scenery, an approximately upright outward view, and pinhole optics are assumptions. Moving traffic, walls, window reflections, slopes, blur, lens distortion and camera stabilization can fool these checks.

Without a measured ruler, metres depend on a broad camera-height prior (median 1.55 m, log standard deviation 0.42). Field of view is also uncertain (78° ± 12°). These are not measurements. Repeated frames cannot remove that scale uncertainty.

Intervals combine resampled feature fits with persistent scale, lens and model-error samples. They are an approximate conditional distribution, not a rigorously calibrated posterior or a 95% real-world coverage guarantee.

An entered height uses an assumed 8% measurement uncertainty. Changing it restarts visual hypotheses. There is no mandatory calibration screen.

Test & export +

Synthetic scenes are rendered into image frames and processed by the same tracker. Their known motion is not sent to the estimator. They test the pipeline, not road accuracy.
