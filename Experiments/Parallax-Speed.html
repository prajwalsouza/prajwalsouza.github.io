Method, assumptions and privacy

This is an experimental automatic estimator, not a validated speedometer. Do not use it for driving decisions. Operate it only while stationary or as a passenger.

There is no manually marked horizon, ground region or camera height. Features throughout the scene are tracked; a robust 3-D camera-motion fit estimates rotation and translation. The camera may look forward, sideways or obliquely. It measures the camera's motion, including hand motion—not necessarily the vehicle's motion. Speed is averaged over a recent window of up to 0.7 seconds; the actual duration is exported.

Metric scale comes from the Metric3D learned depth prior, not measured object sizes. Browser camera intrinsics are unknown: seven diagonal field-of-view hypotheses from 56° to 104° are retained. A shared log-scale uncertainty of 0.35 is an engineering assumption, not a learned guarantee. The displayed 95% mass interval is conditional on these assumptions. It has no validated real-world coverage and is not the complete Bayesian-SLAM design.

Moving foreground objects, window reflections, the vehicle interior, darkness, blur, fisheye lenses and depth-model errors can still produce incorrect readings. Weak tracking and stale estimates produce a dash. Not every failure can be detected. A dominant moving object can defeat the stationary-world assumption.

Model loading and performance depend on the browser. The first use downloads roughly 76–151 MB of model weights, plus runtime assets, from Hugging Face and jsDelivr. Inference runs locally; camera frames are not sent to a server. Those providers receive ordinary asset requests. Model download or device incompatibility leaves the speed unavailable.

GPS is independently displayed and logged; it never enters either estimation worker. Coordinates are discarded immediately. No camera footage is recorded. Logs remain in this tab until exported or cleared by reload. Hiding this tab stops sensors. Reloading loses the session. Browser speed is a noisy reference, not certified ground truth.
